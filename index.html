UrbanHomes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what information the UrbanHomes app (Android package com.company.urbanhomes) and website (urbanhome-ravi.web.app) collect, why, and the choices you have. UrbanHomes is a real-estate video listing service based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Name, email address, profile photo When you create an account or sign in with email or Google To create and identify your account
Mobile number When you choose to add it to your profile, or enter it as a guest viewer So the UrbanHomes team can contact you about properties you are interested in
Location (approximate or precise) Only when you tap a location button (for example "near me" filters), and only after you grant permission To show nearby properties or fill in a location. It is read once, never tracked in the background
App activity: properties viewed, liked, saved, shared, enquiries and reports While you use the app To run the service, show your saved items, and let the UrbanHomes team follow up on interest in their listings
Chat messages, comments and enquiries When you send them To deliver them to the other person
Photos and videos When you upload a profile photo, or when UrbanHomes staff publish a listing To display them in the app
Technical identifiers (an anonymous account ID) Automatically, including for guests To keep your session and preferences working without a login

We do not collect contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone or camera recordings, and we do not use advertising IDs.

2. How information is shared

  • UrbanHomes team: our administrators can see activity on listings (for example "this mobile number viewed your property") so they can respond to interest.
  • Other users: your name and profile photo may be shown next to comments or chats you send. Listing details, including the seller's contact phone, are public so buyers can get in touch.
  • Service providers: data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Hosting) on Google Cloud servers. Google acts as our processor.
  • Legal: if required by law or to protect users' safety.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertising.

3. Security

All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to stored data is restricted by server-side security rules: only you can change your profile, and only UrbanHomes administrators can publish listings or read activity records.

4. Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, profile photo, chats and any listings you own are deleted immediately. Activity records (views, enquiries) may be kept for up to 12 months as business records, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

5. Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Profile → Delete Account and confirm. This permanently removes your account and the data listed above.

By email: if you used the app as a guest, cannot access your account, or want activity records removed, email urbanhomes.hyderabad@gmail.com with the email address or mobile number you used. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

6. Your choices

  • You can browse without creating an account.
  • You can deny or revoke location permission in your phone's settings at any time.
  • You can edit your profile, or ask us for a copy or correction of your data by email.

7. Children

UrbanHomes is intended for adults looking to buy, sell or rent property. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; significant changes will be noted in the app.
